# EXP3
## OBJECTIVE:

Design and develop a detailed 3D solid model of a crankshaft that balances strength, weight, and dimensional accuracy. Develop a complete crankshaft assembly with proper constraints, clearances, and alignment to ensure functional fit with the connecting rod and other engine components. Perform simulations to evaluate the structural integrity and durability of the crankshaft under operating conditions. Finally, prepare the model for prototyping using suitable manufacturing methods and document the complete design, assembly, simulation, and improvement process for presentation.

## DESCRIPTION:

The image shows a drone assembly model developed in 3DEXPERIENCE CATIA Assembly Design. The assembly consists of a top plate, bottom plate, four drone arms, motor mounting areas, spacers/supports, and connecting components. The parts are assembled using appropriate assembly constraints to maintain the required position and alignment. The model represents the overall structure of a quadcopter drone frame.

## CHALLENGES FACED TO DEVELOP:

Developing the drone assembly in 3DEXPERIENCE CATIA involved several challenges. Maintaining accurate alignment between the top plate, bottom plate, and four drone arms was important for achieving a balanced structure. Applying the correct assembly constraints was also challenging because each component had to be positioned properly without unwanted movement. Ensuring dimensional accuracy for the mounting holes, arms, plates, and support components required careful modelling. Another challenge was checking for interference between different components during assembly. The design also needed to maintain sufficient strength and stability while keeping the overall drone frame lightweight. Managing multiple components and making design modifications without affecting the complete assembly required careful use of the 3DEXPERIENCE CATIA Assembly Design environment.
